# Support

## Community support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Praneeth-Suresh/Beryl/issues) for
reproducible bugs and focused feature requests. Search existing issues first,
include the Beryl version or commit, host environment, exact command, and
relevant deterministic-check output.

Community support is provided on a best-effort basis and has no response-time
or resolution commitment.

## Security reports

Do not report suspected vulnerabilities in public issues. Follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Professional services

For paid Beryl setup, migrations, repository-governance design, training,
audits, or ongoing support, see [SERVICES.md](./SERVICES.md).
