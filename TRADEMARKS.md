# Beryl Name and Logo Use

The Apache License, Version 2.0 applies to Beryl's code and documentation. It
does not grant trademark rights. This policy explains the project's current
guidance for use of the Beryl name and logos; it does not claim that any mark
is registered.

## Permitted references

You may use the Beryl name in a truthful, non-misleading way to describe
compatibility, origin, or factual use. For example:

- "Built with Beryl"
- "Compatible with Beryl"
- "A fork of Beryl"

## Uses that need permission

Do not use the Beryl name or logos in a way that suggests your fork, service,
or organization is endorsed by, affiliated with, or an official release of the
Beryl project. In particular, do not name a fork or paid offering in a way
that could confuse users about its source or support relationship.

Do not use the Beryl logo as your organization or product logo without written
permission.
